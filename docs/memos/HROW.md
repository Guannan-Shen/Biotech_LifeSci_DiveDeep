## 2. Why it keeps underperforming

1. **Two misses in a row.** Q1 revenue USD 44.2M against about USD 52.6M consensus; the stock
   fell 21% on 2026-05-12 and never recovered. Q2 revenue USD 70.7M landed just under the
   company's own USD 71-81M Q2 guide.
2. **Volume is growing; revenue per script is not.** VEVYE prescriptions and share rose (14.6% of
   branded dry eye in June vs 7.8% a year earlier), but high-deductible patients and copay-card
   use cut net price: an USD 8M gross-to-net hit in Q1. Business rules changed at the end of
   April; the benefit is promised for H2. The market has seen demand without revenue.
3. **A back-end-loaded guide nobody prices in.** Holding the full-year guide after a weak H1
   means H2 must deliver about twice H1's revenue and swing adjusted EBITDA from -14M to about
   +100M, with most of it in Q4. The price says investors assign low odds to that.
4. **Leverage without trailing earnings.** USD 300M of 8.625% notes and USD 84M of cash are
   comfortable if the guide holds (interest under a third of guided EBITDA) and uncomfortable on
   H1's negative EBITDA. No maturity before 2030, so the risk is dilution or a weaker multiple,
   not default.
5. **Wrong side of 2026's flows.** Money chased high-beta AI-bio (XBI about 47% above its
   52-week low on 10-06); HROW, beta 0.24, has no theme. Low beta also explains why it did not
   fall on 10-06: nobody crowded into it.

## 4. Decision rules for the position

- **Keep only at a size whose bear case you can absorb.** A second miss would plausibly repeat
  May's 21% one-day drop and approach the bear value. If a 40-50% loss on the position exceeds
  its risk budget (framework section 9), trim to budget before Q3.
- **Do not add before Q3** (report expected around mid-November, U).
- **Exit or cut if** Q3 revenue is below about USD 100M, the revenue or EBITDA guide is cut, or
  an equity raise or ATM appears.
- **Add only if** Q3 revenue is about USD 105M or more, adjusted EBITDA is clearly positive
  (about USD 25M or more), VEVYE net revenue per prescription rises, and the stock trades up on
  the print.
- **Tax note:** if held at a loss, harvesting before Q3 and rebuying after the 30-day wash-sale
  window is an option; it changes after-tax returns, not the thesis.
