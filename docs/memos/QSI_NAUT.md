## 4. Variant perception

The market treats the pair as one "single-molecule proteomics" trade: both rose about 63% in 21
sessions around HUPO (QSI) and Nature Methods plus HUPO week (NAUT), and both now carry the same
enterprise value. Our view: they carry different risks. QSI's risk is engineering on a known
chemistry, with a management record of hitting most dated product guides and a runway that covers
launch plus a year. NAUT's risk is reagent science plus financing: five consecutive launch-date
misses, a flagship broadscale mode now undated, and a runway that ends before launch plus a year.
The intact-molecule design is scientifically the more distinctive one (proteoform combinations no
digest-first method can see); the business is the less proven one.

## 7. Scenarios to end-2027 (A; post-financing share counts)

Value per share = (business EV + cash at end-2027) / shares at end-2027. Cash runs down about 20M
(QSI) and 14M (NAUT) a quarter for five quarters, plus assumed raises.

| Scenario | QSI | NAUT |
|---|---|---|
| Bear | Proteus slips again or launches weak; 40M raised near 0.90; EV 30M; value **0.45** (40%) | Shipments slip; 60M raised near 1.00; EV 20M; value **0.67** (45%) |
| Base | Launch in H2 2027, early placements; 50M raised near 1.20; EV 150M; value **0.96** (45%) | Ships H2 2027 with three to five assays; 75M raised near 1.60; EV 150M; value **1.55** (40%) |

The bull cases carry most of the value in both. At today's prices the market is already weighting
them near 25%, a level the evidence (one dated technical win for QSI, none for NAUT since the tau
paper) does not yet support.

## 8. Falsifiers

- QSI bear case falsified by: all 20 residues shown by 2026-12-31 and Proteus beta-site data with
  run-level alignment counts before launch. Bull case falsified by: any further Proteus date change,
  or a Q3 10-Q showing heavy ATM sales below 1.20.
- NAUT bear case falsified by: AKT1 early access on time and a disclosed Voyager pre-order count in
  H1 2027, with a raise done at or above the current price. Bull case falsified by: shipments moved
  past 2027, or a raise below 1.00.

## 9. Runway

- QSI: guided into Q4 2028 (G); on our burn estimate about 50M of cash at end-2027 before any ATM
  use, enough for launch plus about two quarters. Passes the event-plus-12-months screen on guidance.
- NAUT: guided into Q1 2028 (G); about 45M at end-2027 before the planned raise. Fails the
  event-plus-12-months screen; the raise is a certainty, the price is the variable.

## 11. Action

**Watch, not candidate, for both.** Each fails three gates today: business quality `unproven`, H1
dilution flag on (live ATM after a large run-up), and a probability-weighted value below the price.
Triggers to revisit:

- QSI: all 20 residues demonstrated and a Proteus specification sheet, with the stock back near an
  enterprise value of about 50M or less (price about 0.90 at today's share count, A); or a launch
  on time with orders disclosed.
- NAUT: the 2027 financing completed (removes the overhang) and AKT1 early access delivered on time;
  revisit at the post-raise share count.
- For any holder: exit on a SEAR of -2 or worse with volume confirmation at the Q3 report, or on
  any new timeline slip (H17 long-only rule). Do not add into no-news spikes.

Risk budget if either becomes a candidate: event-sized (framework section 9), stress case -80%,
cap about 0.6% of the portfolio per name and about 1% for the pair, because their factor exposure
(single-molecule proteomics theme, retail attention, ATM supply) is shared.
