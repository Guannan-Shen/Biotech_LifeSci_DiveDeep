## Findings worth remembering
- Measured 2021-2026 takeout hazard for listed therapeutics: micro 0.6%, small 4.8%, mid 8.1% a
  year; pooled under USD 25B 2.6%. Size matters about 14-fold from micro to mid.
- The listing exits found 46 deals from 2021-2026 that the 10-08 table lacked, including Arcellx,
  Centessa, RAPT, Soleno, KalVista, Esperion, Astria, Theravance, XOMA and CureVac.
- Ticker reuse is common (RNA, RXDX, CCXI, TBIO, RDUS, FACT, ANAC); Nasdaq industry labels flicker;
  renames that change name and ticker look like exits.
- Era shifts (strategic deals; older rows from recall): CVRs 3% of deals in 2005-2012 to 41% in
  2025-2026; public contests 24% to 1-5%; approved-product targets 61% to about 45%.
- Pilot: three shapes of sale. Buyer-initiated single-bidder negotiations leave no public trace
  inside the process (Crinetics, 114 days, no leak); target-run auctions follow data (Ventyx shopped
  itself to 16 pharmas after Phase 2); activist pressure plus a dated loss of exclusivity preceded
  the Pacira sale by a year.
- XBI excludes GICS Pharmaceuticals, so specialty pharma watchlist names (HROW, ETON) are probably
  outside the benchmark (verify with the holdings file, Q-038).
