framework; add a post-earnings and post-announcement drift (PEAD) strategy and apply it to both;
compare the two, especially technology and product: why QSI digests proteins into peptides before

## Findings worth remembering
- Technology: QSI digests because N-terminal degradation needs free, unblocked front ends (most
  human proteins are N-terminally acetylated) and because read fidelity decays with length. The
  cost is proteoform connectivity and protein inference. NAUT's intact design keeps proteoform
  combinations (tau: 130 groups) but is not sequencing, depends on probe and antibody performance,
  and has not been shown to preserve native complexes (Q-047).
- Credibility: QSI 2 of 3 dated guides hit; NAUT 0 of 5 with a mean slip of about nine months.
- Both now trade at about USD 117M enterprise value after +63% in 21 sessions, with live ATMs (H1
  flag on). Probability-weighted values sit 15-28% below the prices; both are watch, not candidate.
- Drift: 38 of 45 announcements were neutral; timeline slips that cut scope drifted down (NAUT
  2025-02 and 2026-07); QSI's positive no-news spikes reversed in 9 of 11 cases.
- Mirror timing changed on 2026-09-24. The 2026-10-06 cohort snapshot and D-022 are consistent
  with the lag regime; earlier history is not lagged.
