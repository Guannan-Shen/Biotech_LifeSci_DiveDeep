# Session 2026-10-12: insider and holder flow (H18, M17)

## Goal
Investor request: track insider buying and selling and the activity of good biotech-specialist
funds, and find the consequences and patterns that follow. Investor's priors: insider buying does
not always lift a stock; block selling after a big rally marks a stock as somewhat overvalued even
if it does not fall at once.

## Findings worth remembering
- In sample (events 2021-12 to 2023-12, 141 with controls), a heavy-volume down day after a
  doubling lagged run-matched controls by 12.2% at 126 sessions (90%: -19.3% to -6.0%) and 24.1%
  at 252, with **nothing at 20 sessions** and a small bounce at 10. The investor's "not
  immediately" is the measured shape.
- Controls that doubled without a supply day did not mean-revert in this sample (+1.4% at 126).
- Micro caps carry most of it (-20%); small -7%, mid -10% (wide intervals).
- All 18 cells have negative medians; only the base cell is significant on the mean after Holm.
  The mean is fragile because of a right tail of later winners, several of them takeouts (RETA,
  CBAY, ETNB). Exit rules need the takeout override.
- Share count grew at least 10% within 120 sessions after 48% of events vs 38% of controls: a part
  of the "block selling" is the company raising.
- The mirror's `lastsale` is not split-adjusted (D-051); the first run treated reverse splits as
  20x runs.
