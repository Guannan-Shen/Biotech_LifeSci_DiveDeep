  and clinicaltrials.gov (proxy 403). FMP connector works for per-symbol profiles; batch
  market-cap endpoint is blocked by the plan tier.
- Size check at snapshot: ILMN (41B), TEM (13.4B), TXG (11.9B) and TWST (11.7B) are large
  caps; they stay on the watchlist as AI-data tool references but sit outside the core
  small/mid mandate. EDSA (37M) and QSI (307M) are micro/small.
- The stylized rNPV contradicted my initial prior: a 2-year discovery saving is worth
  about a 10-point Phase 2 success-rate gain for mid-value assets (D-012).
