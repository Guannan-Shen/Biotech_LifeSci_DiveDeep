## Findings worth remembering
- Range position before the day (252-day RSV) beat run size: rho -0.60 (p 0.004, Holm-adjusted
  0.024) vs -0.45 (Holm 0.093), n = 23. Inside the launch layer it explains nothing (-0.15):
  stretch is a hazard only inside a crowded theme.
- P/S did not predict the day (rho -0.16) but frames the price: 20x sales needs about 32% a year
  for five years to return 0% at a 5x exit. TXG has the widest growth gap (16.5x on 2-5% growth,
  -23 points); IOVA's multiple is growth-supported (+43 points).
- The launch layer barely moved (median launch +0.1%, commercial -1.7%) and is cheap per unit of
  growth (PSG 0.11-0.18 vs 0.40-0.99 in the AI layers). Its discounts are name-specific (LQDA
  court ruling, ADMA guide cut, ARDX payers, HROW H1 shortfall).
- Base rates split by horizon: no-news shocks bounce within a month (Savor 2012), while sectors
  up 100%+ carry about 50% crash odds over two years without reliably lower average returns
  (Greenwood, Shleifer and You 2019). Issuance is the GSY top flag not yet seen.
- Pooling layers manufactures correlation (both-layer PSG rho -0.69 is mostly layer membership).
