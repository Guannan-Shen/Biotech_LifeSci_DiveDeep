# Session 2026-10-08: Business quality gate (product, moat, business model, people)

## Goal
Investor review of the HROW and MRLN memos: the analysis of products and services was missing.
Evaluate the major products and revenue sources, whether they carry a competitive advantage,
whether the business model is normal, good or great, and whether the team can deliver the plan,
including a willingness to sacrifice the short term for the long term. For HROW, VEVYE and
IHEEZO matter most. Check whether the framework already covered this, plan, execute.

## Blueprint check before executing
The framework did not cover it. Its three gates (evidence, survival, price) never asked what a
company sells, whether that is defensible, or whether management delivers; the archetype table
mentioned "capital allocation" once. The HROW memo mentioned VEVYE once and IHEEZO not at all.
This changes the design, so the blueprint (v0.4), the decision log (D-030 to D-033) and the
framework were updated before any code. Direction re-checked after drafting: the gate serves the
XBI mandate through left-tail avoidance (licensor, payer and reimbursement fragility are left-tail
sources) and through better scenario weights; it adds a testable hypothesis (H15) instead of a
decorative score.

## Findings worth remembering
- HROW is two franchises on one chassis (ocular surface: VEVYE, TYRVAYA, BYQLOVI, compounding;
  retina: IHEEZO, biosimilars, TRIESENCE). VEVYE has a narrow, stable moat and failed the price
  test in H1; IHEEZO is a reimbursement product whose moat is eroding (pass-through ended
  2026-04-01; 2025 revenue inflated by channel loading). TRIESENCE is the best moat per dollar.
- HROW business model: good at the bottom edge (9/16, pricing power 0); deal sourcing is the real
  capability (VEVYE about 11x 2025 revenue per dollar paid; TYRVAYA bought for USD 30M upfront).
- HROW people: 2 of 7 guides hit, posterior 0.36 (0.19-0.55), signed error -4.3%: discount the
  guidance, not the strategy. The 2026 sales build is the one pre-committed sacrifice; checked in
  March 2027.
- H2 bridge (A): no plausible VEVYE x IHEEZO cell reaches the USD 350M low end; re-weighting the
  memo's scenarios under the credibility cap moves the value from about 32 to about 29 vs 30.35.
- MRLN: model unproven (revenue about 7% of cost base), moat none to narrow and eroding on the
  civil side after the NZ withdrawal, people unknown; the target model would be great if
  certification arrives.
- TYRVAYA acquisition (closed H2 2026) and the OPUVIZ January 2027 US launch window were new to
  the repo.
